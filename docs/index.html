🔒 Official Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026
App Version: 4.0

At Swipe Clean, your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict commitment to zero cloud data collection and local storage protection.

1. Zero Data Collection

Swipe Clean operates 100% locally on your Android device. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal data, photos, videos, file names, location data, or analytics with third parties.

2. Device Media Permissions

The app requests access to your device media library solely to:

  • Render local photos and videos in your sorting sessions.
  • Allow you to review items and perform user-approved deletions via official Android system APIs.

No media files are ever uploaded or transmitted off your device.

3. On-Device Sandboxed Storage

All decision histories (Keep, Trash, Skip), session progress, and preference settings are stored exclusively in a sandboxed, encrypted local SQLite (Room) database on your device.

4. No Ads or Third-Party Trackers

Swipe Clean contains no advertisements, no tracking SDKs, no behavioral monitoring, and no telemetry analytics libraries.
